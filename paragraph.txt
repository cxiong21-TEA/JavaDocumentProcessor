Last weekend I decided to reorganize my home office, which had slowly
turned into a chaotic mountain of papers, cables, and half-finished
ideas. I started by sorting old notes into neat little stacks, but I
quickly realized that many of them were from projects I had
compleatly forgotten about. As I sifted through the mess, I found a
notebook filled with random thoughts, sketched diagrams, and a few
recipies I had meant to try. It was both embarassing and amusing to
see how many plans I had enthusiastically begun and then abandonded.
By the afternoon, the desk was finally visible again, though not
quite as spotless as I had imaginned. I even discovered a missing
charger that I had blamed on my kids for months, which made me feel
slightly guility. In the end, the room felt calmer and more inviting,
even if a small pile of important documents still lingered in the
corner, waiting to be actualy dealt with another day.
